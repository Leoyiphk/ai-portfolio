# Editorial checklist (every article)

- [ ] Every fact, regulation, price and statistic checked against a primary source
- [ ] Nothing invented or out of date (Claude is prone to both)
- [ ] At least one real detail from my actual operations that a generic article wouldn't have
- [ ] Written for one clear search intent and one target keyword
- [ ] Traditional Chinese reviewed for Hong Kong wording (not Taiwan or mainland usage), if applicable
- [ ] Title, meta description, headings and internal links done
- [ ] No customer-identifying details
- [ ] Read aloud once: does it sound like a person who knows logistics?
