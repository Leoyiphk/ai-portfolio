# Post approval workflow

1. **Idea:** pick a pillar and a topic (from the weekly trend scan or a question from a consultation).
2. **Draft:** Claude drafts in the chosen language voice.
3. **Check:** I verify every claim and feature name; I remove anything generic.
4. **Add me:** I add a real example or personal angle.
5. **Approve:** I publish or schedule it myself. Nothing is auto-posted.
6. **Review (weekly):** which pillar performed? What questions did people ask? Update the calendar.

## Do not post
- Unverified claims about features or pricing
- Anything about my employer
- Client details without written permission
