Three projects showing how I scope a business problem, build with Claude, **measure whether it worked**, and help non-technical people adopt it.

> Senior data analyst and former SAP business analyst who helps non-technical teams adopt AI, with measured results, in English and Cantonese.

## How every case study is written

1. **Problem**: who has it and what it costs them
2. **Approach**: what I built with Claude and why
3. **Result**: numbers, before vs after
4. **What failed and what I changed**: required, never skipped
5. **Artifacts**: prompts, skills, rubrics, templates
